Carton marking

Mark every carton

  1. Client codeThe code your customer received from us, for example CP-1024. Large letters on two sides of the carton.
  2. Carton numberCarton number and total count, for example 3/40.
  3. Gross weight and dimensionsWeight in kg and size in cm.
  4. Marketplace labels, if providedIf your customer sent barcodes or labels, attach them to every unit before packing.
Documents

Send with the goods

  • Commercial invoice with price per item
  • Packing list: contents, weight and volume of each carton
  • Copy of the sales contract, if your customer requested official customs clearance
